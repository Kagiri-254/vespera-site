Cookie notice

Effective 1 November 2026

This website does not set cookies, and it does not use similar technologies to recognise you between visits.

What that means

There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. The site has no analytics, no advertising and no embedded content from social networks. Its typefaces and images are served from our own domain.

Links that leave the site

When you follow a link to an app store or another site, that site may set its own cookies under its own notice.

If this changes

If we ever add a cookie, we will list it here with its purpose and lifetime before it is set, and ask for consent where the law requires it.
