How we work

Five rules every product is held to.

  • 01

    Privacy by default

    We collect what a product needs to work and nothing more. We do not sell data, build profiles for advertisers or use dark patterns to widen permissions. This is a design constraint we apply before the first line of code, not a policy we write afterwards.

  • 02

    Calm by design

    A product that demands constant attention has failed at its job. We send a notification only when it serves the person in that moment, remove friction that serves no purpose, and build for the session that ends rather than the one that never does.

  • 03

    Honest pricing

    We charge a fair price for a complete product. No artificial limits designed to force an upgrade, no hidden fees and no surprise charge at the end of a trial.

  • 04

    Maintenance is part of the product

    Shipping is the beginning. We fix faults, update for new operating system releases before support lapses and answer support questions. A product we have abandoned is a product we have broken, and we do not do that.

  • 05

    Specific over general

    A product that tries to serve everyone serves no one well. We define a precise audience and a precise problem before we build, and the scope is set in the brief rather than discovered during development.
